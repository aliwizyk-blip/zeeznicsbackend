How Planetary Data Helps Us Understand Space

Planetary science goes beyond images. Comparing mass, diameter, gravity, and density,
we gain insight into how planets form, behave, and interact within the solar system.


Each planet in our solar system has unique physical characteristics. Visual comparisons help
highlight how vastly different terrestrial planets are from gas giants and ice giants.
